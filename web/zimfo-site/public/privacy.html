The short version

Your questions and reading stay private. Zimfo does not send query text, article titles, ZIM filenames, voice recordings, speech transcripts, or precise location through its analytics and diagnostic systems.

Zimfo is an on-device AI application for searching and discussing offline archives. The core language model and content processing run locally. You do not need a Zimfo account to use the app.

Information that stays on your device

The following information is processed locally and is not collected by Tiltas Technologies through Zimfo telemetry:

  • Your typed questions, prompts, and conversation text.
  • Your voice recordings and speech-recognition transcripts.
  • Your Core Location coordinates used for nearby search and routing.
  • Wikipedia article titles, sections, passages, and search terms.
  • ZIM filenames, archive contents, bookmarks, and downloaded model files.

These items may remain in app storage or system-managed caches on your device until you remove them, reset the app, or uninstall it. Device backups are controlled by your Apple and device settings.

Limited diagnostics and usage telemetry

Zimfo uses Google Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, and Performance Monitoring to understand reliability and improve responsiveness. These services may process:

  • Broad query category, such as fact, explanation, article discussion, nearby place, or route request.
  • Broad archive type used, such as Wikipedia or StreetZIM—never an archive filename or article title.
  • Timing measurements for routing, retrieval, model startup, generation, speech, and other performance stages.
  • App version, operating-system version, device class, language, coarse region, session events, and feature use.
  • Crash reports, stack traces, memory state, and related technical diagnostics.

These records may use pseudonymous installation or session identifiers generated by Firebase. We do not use Zimfo telemetry for advertising or sell personal information.

AnalyticsCounts broad feature and query categories without question text or content identifiers.
CrashlyticsReports crashes and technical context needed to diagnose them.
PerformanceMeasures named stages and durations without query text, titles, filenames, transcripts, or GPS coordinates. Firebase may derive coarse country or region information from the request IP address.

Firebase is operated by Google. See Firebase Privacy and Security for more information about its data practices.

Downloads and external services

Zimfo lets you open archives and models acquired from other providers. When you visit or download from Kiwix, StreetZIM, Apple, or another website, that provider may receive ordinary network information such as your IP address, browser details, and download request under its own privacy policy. Those downloads are separate from Zimfo’s on-device conversations.

Wikipedia content is provided through Kiwix-compatible archives. Map data may include OpenStreetMap-derived content packaged by StreetZIM. Zimfo is not affiliated with Wikimedia Foundation, Kiwix, OpenStreetMap, or StreetZIM.

Retention, security, and your choices

Telemetry is retained according to the configured Firebase service retention periods and only as long as reasonably needed for product analytics, crash diagnosis, security, and performance improvement. We use safeguards appropriate to the limited technical data collected, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

You can remove locally stored conversations, archives, and models using controls in the app or your device’s storage settings. Uninstalling Zimfo removes its local app container subject to Apple’s backup and system behavior.

You may contact us to ask a privacy question or request deletion of information that can reasonably be associated with you. Because we do not require an account and use pseudonymous telemetry, we may be unable to identify a particular record.

Privacy choices

You can deny or revoke Microphone, Speech Recognition, and Location permissions in Apple System Settings. Those permissions control local features and do not change Firebase diagnostics. The current beta does not yet include a separate Firebase telemetry switch; contact us with questions or requests.

Children’s privacy

Zimfo is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review and address it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Zimfo develops. We will post the revised policy here and change the effective date. Material changes will be communicated through the app or beta notes when appropriate.
