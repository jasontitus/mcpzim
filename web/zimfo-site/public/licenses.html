Wikipedia and Kiwix archives

Zimfo does not bundle Wikipedia. Users select and download compatible ZIM archives from Kiwix or another provider. Wikipedia text is generally available under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike License and may also be available under the GNU Free Documentation License. Individual media and archives can have additional terms; check the archive and source article for the exact license and attribution.

Kiwix, openZIM, Wikipedia, and Wikimedia are independent projects.

OpenStreetMap and StreetZIM

StreetZIM packages may contain data © OpenStreetMap contributors. OpenStreetMap data is available under the Open Data Commons Open Database License. See OpenStreetMap copyright and attribution for details.

StreetZIM packages regional offline maps and search or routing data. Review the notice supplied with the particular archive you download because coverage and included datasets may differ.

Language and speech models

Zimfo can download or use language-model and speech-model data made available by third parties. The license, acceptable-use terms, attribution requirements, and geographic availability vary by model. The model picker or download source identifies the selected model; review its source page and bundled notice before use or redistribution.

Availability in Zimfo does not transfer ownership or grant rights beyond the model publisher’s license.

Open-source software notices

Zimfo includes open-source libraries under their respective licenses. The authoritative version-specific notices are included with the application and its source distribution. They may include components from Firebase, llama.cpp, Kiwix/openZIM tooling, and Apple-platform packages used for local inference, retrieval, audio, and user interface support.

For a question about a particular component or build, email support@tiltastech.com.
