What to expect

Local by design

Your model and archives run on your device. That makes Zimfo useful offline, but performance depends heavily on your hardware.

Large downloads

Models and archives can require many gigabytes. Downloads can take time and may need the app to remain active.

Variable speed

First requests may build caches, and phones may slow when warm. Some answers can take noticeably longer than cloud AI.

Occasional wrong turns

Zimfo may choose the wrong article, miss conversational context, or produce an incorrect answer. Source grounding is improving.

What you’ll need

  • A compatible iPhone, iPad, or Apple-silicon Mac with enough memory and free storage.
  • Wi‑Fi for the initial model and archive downloads.
  • A Wikipedia ZIM from the Kiwix library; the no-picture edition is recommended for broad coverage with less storage.
  • Optionally, a state, region, or country archive from StreetZIM for offline places and maps.

Conversations that help us learn

  • Start a topic: “Let’s talk about Mongolia,” then ask several follow-ups that use pronouns and implied context.
  • Specify a source: “Use the Wikipedia article Santa Rosa, California and tell me what it says about the 1906 earthquake.”
  • Try ambiguous names and confirm whether Zimfo asks the right disambiguation question.
  • Ask for nearby places and routes with a compatible StreetZIM loaded.
  • Use voice in quiet and moderately noisy environments, and interrupt or continue naturally.
  • Try “Read the full article,” longer answers, and switching between related sections.

Report useful feedback

Send feedback through TestFlight or email support@tiltastech.com. Include:

  1. Your device model, OS version, and Zimfo version.
  2. Whether you were using iPhone or Mac configuration, and the selected model.
  3. The broad kind of archive loaded and approximate size. Do not send filenames if you prefer not to.
  4. What you tried, what happened, and what you expected.
  5. Relevant diagnostic logs, after reviewing them for information you do not want to share.

Firebase diagnostics help us see broad query categories, timings, crashes, and the type of archive involved. They do not include query text, article titles, ZIM filenames, transcripts, or precise location. See the privacy policy.

Beta and safety caveats

Zimfo can be wrong. Do not rely on it as the sole source for medical, legal, financial, emergency, or safety-critical decisions. Offline map data may be incomplete or outdated. Always follow road signs, current conditions, and official guidance.

When driving, prepare downloads and permissions before moving. Use hands-free voice only where lawful, do not interact with the screen, and stop using the app if it becomes distracting.
