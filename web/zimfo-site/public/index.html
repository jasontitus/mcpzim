Simple offline setup

Choose what you want to carry.

Zimfo does not bundle a giant encyclopedia or map. You decide which archives live on your device.

  1. Step 1

    Install Zimfo

    Join the TestFlight beta, install the app, and choose an on-device language model that fits your hardware.

  2. Step 2

    Add Wikipedia

    Download a Wikipedia ZIM from the Kiwix library. The no-picture edition saves substantial storage.

  3. Step 3

    Add offline maps

    Visit StreetZIM to download a map archive for your state, region, or country, then open it in Zimfo.

Designed to know less about you.

Zimfo uses bounded Firebase diagnostics to improve reliability and performance, but never sends query text, article titles, ZIM filenames, voice recordings, transcripts, or precise location.

  • Questions stay on device
  • Voice and transcripts stay on device
  • GPS coordinates stay on device
  • Your archives stay on device
